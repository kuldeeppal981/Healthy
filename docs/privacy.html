Smartfitly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Smartfitly is a health and fitness app (calorie diary, barcode and label scan, allergy alerts, workouts, AI coach). This page explains what data the app uses and why. Smartfitly gives general guidance only. It is not medical advice.

Who we are

Smartfitly is run by an individual developer in India. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

What we collect and why

  • Account: email address and name, to sign you in and sync your data between phones.
  • Profile and health details you enter: age, sex, height, weight, goals, allergies and health conditions you choose to add. Used to calculate calorie targets and allergy and food guidance.
  • Diary and activity: food diary, water, workouts, weight log, streaks. Stored on your phone and, when you are signed in, in your private cloud account.
  • Health Connect (optional): if you allow it, we read steps, heart rate, sleep and exercise from Android Health Connect to show them in the app. We do not write to Health Connect and do not share this data with anyone.
  • Camera: for barcode scan, label photos, meal photos and exercise form check. Live form check and photo form check run on your phone.
  • Microphone: only for voice food logging and voice commands you start.
  • Notifications: reminders and friend alerts. A device token is stored so push notifications can reach your phone.
  • Friends and sharing (optional): friends see only what you choose to share (name, streak, level, achievements). You can turn sharing off at any time.
  • Feedback and Pro interest: messages you send from Help and Feedback, and your answers if you tell us you are interested in a future Pro plan.

AI features

When you use the AI coach, voice logging, meal photo or label photo scan, the text or photo you send is processed by Google Gemini to produce an answer. Please do not send photos of people or documents. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Shared product list

Ingredient text read from product labels may be saved in a shared product list without your name, so the next person scanning the same product gets a faster answer.

Where data is stored

Your account data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, with access rules so each user can reach only their own data. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for storage, Google for AI processing and push notifications) and only to provide the features you use.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • In the app go to Settings to delete all your data or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your data from our servers.
  • You can turn off camera, microphone, notification and Health Connect permissions in your phone settings at any time.
  • You can also email us at the contact address above to ask for deletion.

Children

Smartfitly is not meant for children under 13.

Health disclaimer

Everything in the app is general guidance, not medical advice. If you have a serious allergy, always check the real product label. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
