## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)
Smartfitly is a free health app made for India.

- Calorie diary with Indian foods, katori and roti portions
- Barcode and label scan with allergy alerts
- Voice and photo food logging
- Workout planner and exercise library with yoga and stretches
- Live camera form check with voice guidance and a form score
- AI coach for general food and workout guidance
- Streaks, XP, friends and challenges
- Steps and Health Connect support
- Reports you can save as PDF

Allergy alerts, barcode scan and the basic diary stay free.
Smartfitly gives general guidance only and is not medical advice. Always check the real product label if you have a serious allergy.

## Data safety (draft answers, verify in Console)
Collected: email, name, health info (profile, allergies, diary), fitness info (workouts, steps, heart rate, sleep via Health Connect), photos (meal, label), audio (voice logging), user IDs, app interactions.
Shared with third parties: AI processing by Google Gemini (photos or text you send); push via Firebase. Not sold, not used for ads.
Encrypted in transit: yes. User can request deletion: yes (in app and by email).
